# COOKIE POLICY

## carloslb.com

### What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website stores in the visitor's browser to remember data between visits: preferences, sessions, statistics or advertising, depending on their purpose.

### Does this website use cookies?

**No.** This Website does not use cookies of any kind: neither first-party nor third-party, nor analytics, nor advertising cookies. For this reason, you will not find any cookie consent banner here: there is nothing to consent to.

### Local storage (localStorage)

This Website uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) for a single purpose: **to remember the light or dark theme preference** that you yourself choose by pressing the corresponding button.

This storage is technical in nature, is activated only at your request, contains no personal data, is not shared with anyone and never leaves your browser. In accordance with the criteria of the Spanish Data Protection Agency on the storage of preferences chosen by the user, it is exempt from prior consent.

You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The only consequence will be that the theme will revert to your system preference.

### Third-party services

If you use the contact form, upon submitting it you will be redirected to a page of the provider Form.taxi, where the anti-abuse verification (Friendly Captcha) takes place. Those pages belong to their respective owners and are governed by their own policies:

- Form.taxi: <https://form.taxi/en/privacy>
- Friendly Captcha: <https://friendlycaptcha.com/legal/privacy-service-users/>

Likewise, outbound links on this Website (for example, to GitHub or LinkedIn) take you to third-party sites with their own cookie and privacy policies, unrelated to this Website.

### Changes to this policy

Should this Website incorporate cookies or other tracking technologies in the future (for example, social media sharing buttons), this policy would be updated before their deployment and, where required by law, the corresponding consent would be requested.
